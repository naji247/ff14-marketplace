# Famfrit Gathering Market

A market dashboard for Miner and Botanist gathering on the Famfrit world (Primal data center) in Final Fantasy XIV.

- Lists every gatherable Miner/Botanist item with its gathering level, filtered to your levels.
- Sorts by actual purchases in the last 24 hours, units sold, median price, and gil traded.
- Click an item for a suggested list price, listings, 7-day sales, and take-home after city tax.

Market data comes live from the [Universalis API](https://docs.universalis.app/) (v2). Gathering levels come from the game data tables in [xivapi/ffxiv-datamining](https://github.com/xivapi/ffxiv-datamining).
